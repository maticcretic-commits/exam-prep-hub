03 / library

Study Material

02 / LIBRARY

The revision material that earns marks.

Use these as compact recall sheets. Read one module, cover it, reproduce the rules, then solve questions without looking back.

Arithmetic core

  • Percentage: x% of y = y% of x. Successive changes a%, b% give net a + b + ab/100.
  • Profit & loss: Profit% = profit ÷ cost price × 100. Discount always applies to marked price.
  • Ratio: if A:B = m:n, write A=mk and B=nk before using the total.
  • Average: total = average × count. Replacement changes total by new − old.
  • Mixture: allegation ratio = (dearer − mean):(mean − cheaper).
Speed rule: memorise fractions 1/2 to 1/20 as percentages; they appear inside almost every arithmetic shortcut.

Time & work shortcut

If A finishes in 12 days and B in 18, take total work as LCM 36 units. Rates are 3 and 2 units/day; together they need 36/5 = 7.2 days.

Geometry, algebra & data interpretation

Geometry essentials

  • Triangle angles sum to 180°. Exterior angle = sum of opposite interior angles.
  • Pythagoras: a²+b²=c². Memorise 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17.
  • Circle: circumference 2πr; area πr²; angle in a semicircle is 90°.
  • Cylinder volume πr²h; cone volume ⅓πr²h; sphere volume 4/3πr³.

DI discipline

  • Read units and base year before calculating.
  • Approximate only after checking answer-option gaps.
  • Percentage change = difference ÷ original, not new value.
  • For pie charts, 1% = 3.6° and 25% = 90°.

Series & analogy

  • For number series, inspect first differences, then second differences, ratios, alternating terms and squares/cubes.
  • For letter series, map A=1…Z=26 and test constant or alternating shifts.
  • In analogy, state the relation in words before checking options: “tool → function”, “part → whole”, “cause → effect”.
Do not overfit. SSC series usually use the simplest rule that explains every term.

Logic toolkit

  • Syllogism: draw minimum-valid Venn diagrams; never assume converse.
  • Venn counting: A∪B = A+B−A∩B.
  • Coding: check reversal, alphabet position, pair sums (A+Z=27) and word-length first.
  • Direction: draw north upward and track net horizontal/vertical displacement.
  • Blood relation: assign generation levels (+1 parent, 0 peer, −1 child).

Fast visual reasoning

For mirror images, left and right reverse but top and bottom stay. For water images, top and bottom reverse. In paper folding, mentally unfold in exact reverse order; every fold mirrors existing holes. In embedded figures, trace angles and line lengths rather than the whole outline.

Grammar rules that repeat

  • A singular subject takes a singular verb; phrases between subject and verb do not change agreement.
  • “Each/every/either/neither” takes a singular verb.
  • With “not only…but also” and “either…or”, the verb agrees with the nearer subject.
  • Use fewer for countable nouns and less for uncountable quantities.
  • Parallel ideas need parallel forms: “reading, writing and revising”.

Vocabulary engine

  • bene- good: benefit, benevolent
  • mal- bad: malfunction, malevolent
  • cred- believe: credible, incredulous
  • loqu- speak: eloquent, soliloquy
  • chrono- time: chronology, synchronize
Learn every word as: meaning + one collocation + one opposite. Isolated word lists decay quickly.

Comprehension method

  1. Read the question stem first; mark whether it asks fact, inference, tone or vocabulary.
  2. For factual questions, choose only what the passage states.
  3. For inference, reject options that add a new claim or use absolute words such as “always”.
  4. For cloze tests, check grammar, meaning and collocation—in that order.

Polity & history spine

  • The Constitution was adopted on 26 Nov 1949 and came into force on 26 Jan 1950.
  • Fundamental Rights are in Part III; Directive Principles in Part IV; Fundamental Duties in Part IVA.
  • Money Bills can be introduced only in Lok Sabha; the Speaker certifies them.
  • 1857: major uprising; 1885: INC founded; 1919: Jallianwala Bagh; 1930: Dandi March; 1942: Quit India.

Science & geography spine

  • SI units: force—newton; power—watt; pressure—pascal; charge—coulomb.
  • Vitamin C deficiency: scurvy; D: rickets; iron deficiency: anaemia.
  • Tropic of Cancer passes through eight Indian states.
  • Western Ghats intercept the southwest monsoon; rain-shadow lies east of the range.
  • Earth rotates west to east, causing sunrise to appear in the east.

Economy basics

  • Repo rate: rate at which RBI lends short-term funds to banks against securities.
  • Inflation: sustained rise in the general price level; CPI tracks retail inflation.
  • Fiscal deficit: total expenditure minus total non-debt receipts.
  • GDP measures final goods and services produced within a country in a period.

Computer basics

  • CPU includes control unit and arithmetic logic unit; RAM is volatile memory.
  • 1 byte = 8 bits. An operating system manages hardware and software resources.
  • HTTPS encrypts browser–server traffic; phishing impersonates a trusted source.
  • Spreadsheet formulas begin with =; absolute cell references use $ signs.

Syllabus extras

Two official-syllabus topics that need their own cards: trigonometry and elementary statistics.

Trigonometry quick reference

  • sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ; 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
  • sin 30°=1/2, sin 45°=1/√2, sin 60°=√3/2; cos mirrors sin; tan 45°=1
  • sin(A+B)=sinA cosB + cosA sinB; cos(A−B)=cosA cosB + sinA sinB
  • For right triangles: opposite/hypotenuse = sin, adjacent/hypotenuse = cos, opposite/adjacent = tan

Elementary statistics

  • Mean = sum of observations ÷ count; median = middle value of ordered data
  • Mode = most frequent value; range = max − min
  • Adding a constant k to every value shifts mean and median by k, leaves range unchanged
  • Multiplying every value by k multiplies mean, median and range by k

05 / plan

Study Plan

03 / PLAN

Six weeks. One repeatable rhythm.

Each week follows the same loop: concept → timed set → error log → spaced revision. Allocate more time to the two weakest subjects after the diagnostic.

WEEK 01

Baseline

One full diagnostic. Arithmetic fundamentals, grammar agreement, basic series and static-GK map.

WEEK 02

Core build

Percentage/ratio, coding/syllogism, parts of speech, polity and modern history.

WEEK 03

Expand

Time-work/speed, visual reasoning, vocabulary roots, geography and science.

WEEK 04

Exam mode

Algebra/geometry, mixed reasoning, comprehension, economy and computer basics.

WEEK 05

Pressure

Three sectional tests and two full mocks. Re-solve every wrong and guessed answer.

WEEK 06

Convert

Five full mocks, formula/vocabulary recall, current-affairs revision and sleep discipline.
