1 · How to apply for a government job — step by step

  1. Read the official notification PDF — never apply from a forwarded message or a screenshot. Check the advertisement number, last date, and the applying authority's website address.
  2. Check you meet the eligibility — qualification, age on the cut-off date, and (for state jobs) domicile/language requirements.
  3. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if the body requires it (SSC, UPPSC, TNPSC, RPSC via SSO all mandate OTR before you can fill any form).
  4. Fill the online form carefully — name, date of birth and category must match your certificates exactly. A mismatch discovered at document verification can cancel your candidature.
  5. Upload documents in the specified size and format (photo, signature, certificates). Wrong formats are the most common reason forms get rejected.
  6. Pay the fee only through the official payment gateway inside the form. Save the receipt/registration number. No genuine recruitment asks for money over UPI, phone calls or private accounts.
  7. Save and print the submitted form. Note the correction window dates, if any.
  8. Track the lifecycle on the official site: admit card → exam → answer key → result → document verification → final selection.

2 · Documents checklist

  • Recent passport-size photograph (as per notification specs)
  • Signature on white paper (scanned, correct size)
  • Matriculation / 10th certificate (proof of date of birth)
  • 12th / graduation / PG marksheets and degree certificates
  • ITI / diploma / professional qualification certificates, if applicable
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS) in the current central/state format, valid as on the cut-off date
  • Domicile / residence certificate and language/dialect proof (many state PSCs require it — e.g. Manipuri + Permanent Resident Certificate for MPSC Manipur)
  • Disability certificate (PwD) and ex-servicemen discharge book, if claiming those quotas
  • Experience certificates for posts that demand them (e.g. 3 years administrative translation for MPSC's Assistant Language Director)
  • Professional council registration (NMC/State Medical Council for doctors, nursing councils for nurses)
  • CTET/STET certificate for teaching posts; B.Ed/D.El.Ed certificates
  • Aadhaar + a valid mobile number and email — OTP-based logins are standard now
Common rejection reason: category or domicile certificates issued after the notification's cut-off date. Get them made before you apply, not after the result.

4 · Application fees & reservation

  • Fees typically range ₹100–₹1,200 depending on the exam and your category. Many posts exempt SC/ST/PwD/women entirely; some (e.g. OPSC APP) refund the fee to candidates who actually sit the exam.
  • Reservation follows the notification: central recruitments use central OBC-NCL/EWS/SC/ST lists; state recruitments use that state's lists — a certificate from the wrong list can be rejected.
  • Age relaxation is standard: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC-NCL, 10 years for PwD, plus ex-servicemen concessions — exact figures per notification.
  • Pay the fee before the fee deadline (sometimes a day earlier than the form deadline, e.g. BPSC TRE 4.0: fee by 25.10, form by 26.10.2026).

5 · Typical exam calendars (general guidance)

  • SSC — CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD Constable, JE, CPO run on annual cycles; notices at ssc.gov.in.
  • UPSC — Civil Services notification ~Feb, Prelims ~May; CDS/NDA twice a year; AFCAT twice a year.
  • IBPS — PO/MT, Clerks, Specialist Officers, RRBs: registration windows ~3–4 weeks, exams Aug–Nov.
  • Railways (RRB) — centralised CEN notices (ALP, Technician, NTPC, JE, Group D) on a periodic, not strictly annual, cycle.
  • Teaching — CTET twice a year (CBSE); KVS/NVS drives are irregular; state TRE cycles roughly annual.
  • India Post GDS — usually 2 schedules a year (Jan/Feb and Jul/Aug), merit-list based.
  • AIIMS NORCET — 1–2 times a year for Nursing Officers.
  • ESIC hospitals — rolling walk-ins for Senior/Junior Residents year-round.
  • NAPS apprenticeships — no calendar; establishments post through the year.
These are rhythms, not schedules. Apply only against a live notification with an advertisement number.
